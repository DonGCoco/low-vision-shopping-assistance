# Low Vision Shopping Assistance

Graduation thesis project by **Dong Jieru**, Ritsumeikan University, January 2025.

**Vision Language Model Integrating Image Enhancements and Automatic Speech Recognition**

A multimodal shopping assistance project for people with low vision, combining image restoration, visual question answering and speech interaction.

## Research Motivation and Social Impact

Supermarket shopping involves more than reading labels. Choosing products also requires access to visual information, such as appearance, colour, and visible damage. For people with low vision, difficulty accessing this information can limit independent decision-making and increase reliance on others.

This project explores how image enhancement, vision-language models, and speech interaction can make product information more accessible. Its broader goal is to support autonomy and participation in everyday activities, allowing people with low vision to make more informed shopping decisions based on their own needs and preferences.

## Research question

Can restoring motion-blurred product photographs improve the quality of descriptions generated by a vision-language model?

## Architecture

1. Load a product photograph in the PyQt5 desktop client.
2. Restore the image with DeblurGAN.
3. Pass the restored image and question to Qwen2-VL.
4. Convert the answer to speech through a separate TTS service.
5. Submit follow-up questions through speech recognition.

The system architecture combines DeblurGAN for image restoration, Qwen2-VL for visual understanding, GPT-SoVITS for speech synthesis and Whisper for speech recognition. The desktop client communicates with modular HTTP services.

## Repository layout

- `client/`: original PyQt5 desktop client.
- `server/`: Flask query endpoint, DeblurGAN inference, Whisper ASR and Qwen2-VL integration.
- `deblur/`: recovered DeblurGAN training, inference and preprocessing project.
- `speech/`: standalone speech recognition, recording and gTTS smoke tests.
- `docs/graduation-thesis.pdf`: supplied thesis, primary research record.
- `docs/archive/`: earlier presentation, introduction draft and LaTeX materials.
- `docs/RESULTS.md`: results transcribed from the thesis, with evaluation caveats.
- `docs/SETUP.md`: environment, model configuration and development notes.

## Research data

The thesis reports a 5,500-image ImageNet subset and self-collected Japanese supermarket photographs. It describes 384×384 resizing with aspect-ratio preservation and padding, augmentation, paired synthetic motion blur, and approximately 25,000 images split 80/10/10. 

Datasets and pretrained model checkpoints are obtained separately. The repository contains project source, sample images, the thesis and presentation materials.

## Results reported in the thesis

| Metric | Blurred input | DeblurGAN-restored input |
|---|---:|---:|
| SSIM ↑ | 0.45 | 0.87 |
| PSNR, dB ↑ | 18.2 | 26.5 |
| FID ↓ | 75.3 | 38.7 |
| BLEU ↑ | 0.75 | 0.90 |
| ROUGE-1 ↑ | 0.85 | 0.95 |
| ROUGE-2 ↑ | 0.72 | 0.88 |
| ROUGE-L ↑ | 0.83 | 0.94 |

Results are reported in the [graduation thesis](docs/graduation-thesis.pdf). See [experimental results](docs/RESULTS.md) for restoration baselines and evaluation scope.

## Setup and usage

The implementation uses Python, PyTorch, Flask and PyQt5. Model checkpoints and speech services are configured separately. See [setup and development notes](docs/SETUP.md) for dependencies, expected model paths and startup instructions.

## Project contributions

- Defined supermarket information needs for low-vision users, including appearance and label information.
- Prepared paired image data and synthetic motion blur for restoration experiments.
- Integrated image restoration with visual-language processing and speech interaction.
- Evaluated image quality using SSIM, PSNR and FID, and description quality using BLEU and ROUGE.
- Developed a desktop client to access the processing pipeline.
